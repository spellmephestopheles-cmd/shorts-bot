4. [INDIA/ASIA] Why Japan's Trains Stop for a Single Student

Twist: The station's closing date was chosen by her graduation, not by the accountants.
Virality: 4.1/5 (strongest: emotional payoff + shareability)

Pivot: You see, your brain files a doorway as an event boundary and starts a new memory chapter.
Twist: Walking back to the first room brings the memory back.
Virality: 4.2/5 (strongest: relatability + rewatch)
